Introduction

Math Blitz is a mental arithmetic game published by V Developers ("we", "us"). This page explains how the app handles your information. By using the app you agree to this policy and to the Terms of Use below.

Data we collect

WhenWhat
You sign in with GoogleYour Google account name, email address and profile photo link, plus a Firebase user ID. Your email stays in Firebase Authentication only and is not written to the game database.
You play as a guestAn anonymous Firebase user ID and a generated display name (like Guest_0000). No email or name.
You playDisplay name, avatar or Google profile photo link, total XP, best score, games played and daily streak. For the daily and monthly leaderboards: your games played, top 5 scores and rating for that period.

Detailed match history (question-by-question results), sound and timer settings, and any photo you pick from your gallery as an avatar stay only on your device and are never uploaded. The app requests the Internet, network state and vibration permissions only. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.

How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep your progress when you reinstall or change phones.
  • To show daily and monthly leaderboards.
  • To keep the leaderboards fair.

We never sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Leaderboard visibility

Other signed-in players can see: your display name, avatar or profile photo, games played, top 5 scores and rating.

They cannot see: your email address or your match history.

If you sign in with Google, your Google name and profile photo are used by default. You can change your display name and avatar in your profile, and you can use a preset avatar instead of your photo. You can delete your account at any time (see below).

Data storage and security

Cloud data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and handled by Firebase Authentication. Google encrypts data in transit and at rest. Access rules let each user write only their own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is less to protect.

Data sharing

We share data only with Google (Firebase), which processes it on our behalf to run sign-in, storage and security checks. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Children's privacy

Math Blitz is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Account and data deletion

You can delete your account and cloud data yourself, or ask us to do it.

In the app (instant):

  1. Open Settings from the bottom bar.
  2. Tap Delete Account in the Account card, or Delete Account & Cloud Data under Data & Storage.
  3. Confirm with Delete Permanently Now. If asked, sign in with Google again to confirm it is you.

Deletion happens immediately and is permanent. There is no waiting period and no way to recover your data. We delete your cloud profile, all your leaderboard entries and your sign-in account, and we clear the game records and settings stored on your device.

By email: write to kammarivinaykumar216@gmail.com from the email linked to your account (or tell us your display name for guest accounts). We process email requests within 30 days at the latest.

Uninstalling the app removes local data but does not delete your cloud account. Use one of the options above for that.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have further rights. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.
